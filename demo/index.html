    Which mode is the gateway in? In stub-accept-all the
    Pay button works as-is: payments are accepted without touching the chain and
    no funds move. In sui-grpc the signature is verified
    on chain and the transfer is simulated, so this button's placeholder payment is
    correctly refused — a real payment needs a wallet signature
    (@mysten/dapp-kit's signTransaction, or
    scripts/pay-with-sui-cli.sh). Either way the free tier, the 402
    challenge and the session flow above behave identically.
